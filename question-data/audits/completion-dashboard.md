## Scope gaps

- No versioned NERDC, NECO, BECE or full IGCSE subject syllabuses exist in this checkout. Database-held or tenant-defined specifications were not accessed. Do not invent class-subject cross-products.
- JAMB appears in question data but is absent from EXAM_CATALOG; BECE is in the app catalogue but has no stored question batch. Application behaviour has not been changed.
- WAEC/JAMB supplied compilations list 37 subject labels, with only 33/25 collected respectively. These indexes are candidate scope, not definitive examination subject catalogues.
- WAEC official catalogue distinguishes new 2028 core subjects. Historical labels and trade subjects require examination-year reconciliation before targets become final.
- NERDC supports 16 application class labels; many existing drafts say only Nursery, Primary, JSS or SS. Exact class, adopted curriculum version, term and objectives require source evidence.
- NCEE official 2026 components include National Values Education and vocational aptitude. Existing Basic Science, Quantitative Reasoning and Verbal Reasoning labels need component mapping.
- IGCSE requires qualification code, examination year, tier and assessment objectives. Four observed subject names do not establish the entire intended Cambridge portfolio.
- IELTS Reading/Writing require Academic versus General Training variants. Listening/Speaking need original audio assets and performance rubrics; MCQ writing advice is not a writing task.
- TOEFL must follow the post-21-January-2026 task structure; legacy writing MCQs are not production tasks. SAT has two sections, not historical SAT Subject Tests.
- Objective register covers selected English/Economics objectives only, with paraphrased author proposals. It is not a full syllabus denominator. No coverage percentages or completed banks claimed.
- Runtime verbal/quantitative generators and Flutter worksheet engines are separate assets, not independently reviewed original exam questions. Their generated counts are excluded.
- All targets are provisional until scope, originality, format variety and age-appropriate curriculum capacity are academically justified. No bank has independent academic sign-off.
