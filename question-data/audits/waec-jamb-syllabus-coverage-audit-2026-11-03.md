## Missing coverage and quality findings

1. The syllabus compilations contain 33 WAEC subjects and 25 JAMB subjects with text. Many subjects have no staged questions.
2. WAEC Mathematics requires objective and written-response papers; current staged questions are MCQs only.
3. JAMB Mathematics syllabus includes number bases, polynomials, matrices, loci, calculus, dispersion and other objectives without explicit matching topic labels in the staged JAMB Mathematics questions.
4. Topic labels are not objective identifiers; a declared topic is not proof of syllabus coverage.
5. Some difficulty tags appear overstated relative to one-step questions. Difficulty must be recalibrated per exam and age.
6. Exact prompt uniqueness does not establish conceptual uniqueness or uniqueness against production D1.
7. Explanations and answers require subject-expert verification.
