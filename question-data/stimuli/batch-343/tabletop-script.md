# Original tabletop observation script for ndv-343-013

This is a fictional classroom model, not a threat drill. Requires the unfilled
performance protocol and independent safeguarding/class/rubric review before use.

Prepare a paper rectangle labelled STORE, a small loose paper handle attached
to its front, and a plain paper figure. Explain that this represents a model
neighbourhood store, that no real event is happening, and that the pupil stays
seated. Do not attach ethnicity, clothing, diagnosis or a name to the figure.

Show the sequence once at an accessible pace: move the paper figure across the
desk to STORE; move its paper hand to the handle; pull the paper handle twice;
leave the figure beside STORE. No loud sounds, realistic props or pupils acting
as suspects. Do not state a motive, permission, ownership or guilt.

Then invite the pupil’s own short report to you as the trusted adult. Do not
supply the answer while scoring. A pupil may say they do not know the reason or
permission. Assess the actually observed model action and heard communication,
not memory of the printed model answer. No collection of real-world evidence.

The script/labels are original. Runtime rendering, accessibility adaptation,
actual delivery and independent rubric calibration remain unverified.
